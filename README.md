### What is ManaSetu?

**ManaSetu** is a community-powered micro-task platform that acts as a **bridge between Posters and Taskers**.

A comprehensive ecosystem designed to empower individuals through meaningful work. ManaSetu enables people to:

- 📝 **Post Tasks** - Share opportunities and collaborate with skilled individuals
- 💼 **Earn Income** - Complete tasks and earn through your skills and expertise
- 🤝 **Build Community** - Join communities, collaborate, and grow together
- 🎯 **Create Opportunities** - Access a trusted ecosystem for meaningful connections

**The Mission:** Democratize opportunities and create a platform where skills meet demand, and effort meets reward.
